How to use this

Classification workflow

  • Confirm the destination market and applicable legal regime (notably GB vs Northern Ireland).
  • Search using the exact substance identity and any group/index entry.
  • Apply mandatory entries for the hazard classes they cover; assess remaining hazards.
  • Check SCLs, ATEs, M-factors, notes, and effective or transitional dates.
  • Document the legal source, version, entry and date checked.
What this guide is — and isn't

Interpretation and limits

This chapter covers GHS/CLP hazard classification lists, not chemical inventories, restriction lists, transport dangerous goods schedules or occupational exposure limits.

Some entries represent markets or regulatory areas rather than a single country. A “no general list” label is not an exemption from classification duties.

Sources are linked, not copied. Download the official lists from their publishers to get the current data.
